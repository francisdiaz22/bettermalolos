# LGU handoff protocol

BetterMalolos may prepare an aggregate report for the intended City or barangay office after internal review. The report records the office, delivery method, date, reference number or source document, aggregate methodology, and any verified response.

Acceptable evidence includes a published office contact, written acknowledgement, meeting minutes, or official correspondence reference. An informal view, like, or conversation does not make an office a partner, participant, or responder. A handoff does not imply endorsement, approval, funding, or implementation.
