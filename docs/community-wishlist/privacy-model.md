# Privacy model

Collect only the email needed to verify and operate an account, an optional display name, an optional barangay, and the content needed for a wish. Do not collect full addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, precise map pins, demographic profiles, or sensitive personal information.

Before any account or submission data is sent, show a linked privacy notice covering data collected, purpose, recipients, retention, rights, and contact channel. Submission requires an unchecked affirmative consent checkbox. Store the policy version, consent timestamp, and wording version with the submission.

The default public identity is anonymous. If a resident opts in for a particular wish or support, expose only their chosen display name. Never publish email, account ID, barangay privacy settings, support history, or raw abuse-prevention data. LGU reports use aggregate anonymous figures unless separate explicit consent or a lawful requirement applies.
