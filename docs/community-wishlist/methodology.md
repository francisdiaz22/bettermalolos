# Methodology

The Wishlist is community input, not an election, petition, census, or official City survey. “Supporters” means distinct verified accounts or approved abuse-prevention signals recorded by the system; one account may support a wish once. Raw counts are not, by themselves, Community Priority designations.

Reports should disclose the reporting period, publication status, support-count method, number of barangays represented, duplicate handling, and limitations. Community Priority should consider support count, barangay diversity, sustained support, impact breadth, and local relevance. The final threshold and review process must be published before the designation is enabled.
