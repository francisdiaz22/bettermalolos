# Moderation policy

Every submission is reviewed before publication. The target review time is 48 business hours; this is a target, not a guarantee.

## Publish

Publish constructive proposals about services, infrastructure, places, policies, and community improvements in Malolos. A moderator may improve wording for clarity without changing the submitter's intent. The original remains private for auditability and follows the retention policy.

## Reject or request a rewrite

Reject personal accusations, defamation, political attacks, calls to remove a named person, claims of criminality or corruption, doxxing, commercial advertising, spam, emergencies, law-enforcement reports, individual disputes, and content unrelated to Malolos. Direct urgent matters to the appropriate official channels. Do not publish details that could identify a person through context.

Suggested reason labels are: duplicate; complaint rather than proposal; personal accusation; advertising/spam; outside Malolos; insufficient information; other.

Moderators record the decision and a safe resident-facing explanation. Internal notes, reporter details, legal-risk assessments, and abuse-prevention signals are never public.
