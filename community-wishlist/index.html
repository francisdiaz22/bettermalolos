About this wishlist

This is an independent, resident-led BetterMalolos initiative about possible improvements in Malolos. A support count is community input—not an election, petition, procurement request, or City endorsement. Forwarding an idea in the future would not mean it has been approved or funded by the LGU.

Learn how the Wishlist handles support counts, status changes, moderation, and privacy. The prototype remains read-only while the Phase 0 launch gate is reviewed.
